# Returns Exceptions

Some items have stricter return rules, mostly for safety and hygiene. These rules override the standard 60-day **Returns Policy**.

## Climbing safety gear
Harnesses, climbing ropes, crampons and ice axes **cannot be returned once used**. We cannot verify that used safety gear has not been loaded, dropped or damaged, so we will not resell it.
- Unused climbing safety gear, with tags attached, can be returned within **60 days**.
- Chalk bags and climbing shoes follow the standard Returns Policy.

## Helmets
Bike helmets **cannot be returned once worn outside**, or after any impact, however small. Unworn helmets with tags and packaging can be returned within **60 days**. A helmet that was in a crash may qualify for our **Crash Replacement** discount; see **Warranty Overview**.

## Footwear
Hiking boots, trail shoes, climbing shoes and snowboard boots can be returned within **60 days** only if they have been worn **indoors only**. Shoes with dirt, scuffs or wear on the soles cannot be returned. Try new footwear on carpet first.

## Skis and snowboards
Skis and snowboards can be returned within **60 days** if they have not been ridden. We will refuse a return if the base or edges show scratches, or if binding screw holes have been drilled.

## Goggles
Goggles can be returned within **60 days** if the lens film is intact and the lens has no scratches.

## GPS watches, navigators and bike computers
Navigation electronics have a **30-day** return window from the order date, not 60 days. They must come back with the charger, cables, manuals and original box. Remove any personal data and unpair the device from your account before you send it.

## Final sale items
Items marked **Final sale** on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged. They are still covered by the manufacturer's warranty against defects.

## Defective items
If an item arrives defective, none of these exceptions apply. Contact us within **60 days** of the order date for a free return; see **Warranty Overview**.
