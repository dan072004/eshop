# Warranty Overview

AdventureWorks is a retailer. The products we sell are made by other brands, and each brand's warranty covers manufacturing defects. We help you claim it.

## First 60 days after the order date: we handle it
If an item is defective within **60 days** of the order date, contact us. We will send a free return label and give you a replacement or a full refund, including shipping. You do not need to deal with the brand.

## After 60 days: claim from the brand
After 60 days from the order date, warranty claims go to the brand. You will need your **order number** and **order date**, which are in **My orders**. If a brand does not respond within **30 days**, contact us and we will follow up for you.

## What warranties do not cover
Warranties cover **manufacturing defects**. They do not cover normal wear, accidents, misuse, unauthorised repairs, or gear used for rental or commercial purposes. See **Defects vs Normal Wear** for examples.

## Climbing safety gear
Retire harnesses, ropes and crampons according to the manufacturer's instructions, even if they look fine. Never use climbing gear after a significant fall or impact. A warranty does not extend the safe life of safety gear.

## Crash Replacement for helmets
A helmet that has been in **any impact** must be replaced, even if it looks undamaged. Accidents are not covered by warranty, but within **3 years** of the order date we offer **30% off** a replacement helmet from the same brand. Contact us with your order number and a photo of the damaged helmet.
