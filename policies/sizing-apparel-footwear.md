# Sizing Guide: Jackets and Footwear

Sizes vary between brands. When a product page has its own size chart, use that instead of this guide.

## Jackets: how to measure
- **Chest:** measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms, with the tape level.
- **Sleeve:** measure from the centre back of your neck, over your shoulder, to your wrist, with your arm slightly bent.

## Choosing a jacket fit
- **Between sizes:** choose the larger size if you will layer a fleece or insulated jacket underneath.
- **Shell jackets** are cut to fit over layers. **Insulated jackets** are cut closer to the body.
- Raise your arms above your head: the hem should not lift above your waist, and the cuffs should stay at your wrists.

## Footwear: how to measure
Measure your feet in the **evening**, when they are at their largest. Stand on paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance. Measure both feet and use the longer one.

## Choosing the right footwear size
- **Hiking boots and trail shoes:** go **half a size up** from your everyday shoes. You need about a thumb's width of space in front of your longest toe so your toes do not hit the front on descents.
- **Climbing shoes:** fit snug with toes slightly curled. Many climbers go **half to one full size down** from their street shoe size. Check the product page, because brands differ.
- **Snowboard boots:** fit snug with toes just touching the front when standing. They pack out by about half a size. See **Sizing Guide: Skis and Snowboards**.
- Try footwear on indoors with the socks you will wear outside. Worn footwear cannot be returned; see **Returns Exceptions**.
