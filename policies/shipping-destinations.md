# Shipping Destinations

## Countries we ship to
We ship to the **United States** (all 50 states) and **Canada**. We do not ship to other countries yet.

## United States
- All shipping options are available in the contiguous US.
- **Alaska and Hawaii:** Standard and Expedited only, with **2–4 extra business days**. Next day is not available.
- **US territories** (Puerto Rico, Guam, US Virgin Islands): Standard only, with **5–10 extra business days**.

## Canada
- Standard (**5–10 business days**, $14.95) and Expedited (**3–5 business days**, $29.95). Next day is not available.
- **Free shipping over $75 does not apply** to Canada.
- Prices are charged in **USD**. Canadian **import duties and taxes** are collected by the carrier on delivery and are paid by the customer.
- Skis and snowboards cannot be shipped to Canada.

## PO boxes and military addresses
PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO addresses get **Standard only**. We cannot ship skis or snowboards to them.

## Address accuracy
Check your address before you place the order: we cannot change it afterwards (see **Order Cancellation and Changes**). If a parcel is returned to us because the address was wrong or undeliverable, we refund the items once it arrives, minus the original shipping cost.
