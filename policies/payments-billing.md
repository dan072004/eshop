# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept these credit and debit cards:
- **Visa**
- **Mastercard**
- **American Express (Amex)**

We do not accept PayPal, cash on delivery, bank transfer, cheques or cryptocurrency. AdventureWorks gift cards can be used alongside a card; see **Promotions and Gift Cards**.

## Currency
All prices are in **US dollars (USD)**, including for orders shipped to Canada. Your bank may charge a foreign transaction fee or use its own exchange rate.

## When you are charged
You are **not charged when you place the order**. We first check that every item is in stock. Once stock is confirmed, we take payment, and the order status changes to **Paid**. See **Order Status and Tracking**.

- If an item is out of stock, the whole order is cancelled and **you are not charged**.
- Your bank may show a temporary authorisation when you place the order. If the order is cancelled before payment, it disappears within **3–5 business days**.

## Declined payments
If your card is declined, the order is **cancelled automatically** and you are not charged. Common reasons are an incorrect card number, expiry date or security code, an expired card, or a spending limit. Check your details with your bank and place a new order.

## Sales tax
US sales tax is calculated at checkout from your shipping address and shown before you pay. Orders to Canada are not charged sales tax by us; Canadian import duties and taxes are collected on delivery (see **Shipping Destinations**).

## Payment security
Card details are sent over an encrypted connection and processed by our payment provider. AdventureWorks staff never see your full card number, and we will never ask for it by email, phone or chat.

## Refunds
Refunds always go back to the **original card** within **5–7 business days** after we process them. See **Returns Policy**.
