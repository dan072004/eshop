# Order Status and Tracking

## Where to see your order
Sign in and open **My orders**. The page updates by itself when your order's status changes.

## What each status means
| Status | Meaning | Can I cancel? |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitted** | We have received your order. There is a short grace period in case you change your mind. | Yes |
| **Awaiting validation** | We are checking that every item is in stock. | Yes |
| **Stock confirmed** | Every item is in stock. We are now taking payment. | Yes |
| **Paid** | Payment succeeded. Your order is being packed. | No, return it instead |
| **Shipped** | Your order has left our warehouse. | No, return it instead |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled by you or by us. You have not been charged. | — |

An order normally moves through these statuses in order, from Submitted to Shipped.

## Why an order is cancelled
- **You cancelled it** before it was paid.
- **An item was out of stock.** If even one item is unavailable, the **whole order** is cancelled. Place a new order for the items that are in stock.
- **Payment was declined.** See **Payments and Billing**.

You are **never charged** for a cancelled order.

## Tracking your parcel
When your order is **Shipped**, we email you a tracking link. Tracking can take up to **24 hours** to show the first scan.

## Late, lost or damaged parcels
- If your parcel has not arrived **5 business days** after the latest estimated date, contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier.
- Report a **damaged parcel or missing items within 7 days** of delivery. Keep the box and packaging, and send photos.
- We will send a free replacement or refund you in full, including shipping.
