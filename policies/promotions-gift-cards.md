# Promotions and Gift Cards

## Promo codes
- You can use **one promo code per order**.
- Enter the code at checkout, before you pay. We cannot add a code to an order after it is placed.
- Promo codes cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be used to buy gift cards.
- Each code has its own end date and conditions, shown where the code was offered.

## Returning discounted items
If you return an item from an order that used a promo code, you are refunded **the amount you actually paid** for that item, not its full price.

## Price adjustments
If the price of an item drops within **14 days** of your order date, contact us and we will refund the difference to your original card.
- Price adjustments apply to the AdventureWorks price only, not to competitors' prices.
- They do not apply to promo code discounts, clearance or **Final sale** items.
- One price adjustment per item.

## Gift cards
- AdventureWorks gift cards are available from **$25 to $500**.
- Gift cards **never expire** and have no fees.
- Use a gift card at checkout. If it does not cover the whole order, pay the rest with Visa, Mastercard or Amex.
- Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law, and cannot be replaced if lost or stolen.
- Refunds for items paid with a gift card go back to a gift card.

## Member offers and newsletters
Sign up to the AdventureWorks newsletter for early access to sales. Newsletter offers follow the same rules as promo codes.
