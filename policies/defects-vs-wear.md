# Defects vs Normal Wear

Warranties cover **defects**: faults in materials or workmanship. They do not cover **normal wear**: the expected effect of using gear over time. Use these examples to tell the difference. If you are unsure, contact us with photos.

## Footwear
- **Defect:** soles separating from the upper, eyelets pulling out, seams splitting in the first months of normal use, waterproof boots leaking when new.
- **Normal wear:** worn tread, holes worn at the heel, compressed insoles, scuffed toes, loss of waterproofing after long use.

## Jackets
- **Defect:** zips that fail without force, seam tape peeling, delaminating fabric, faulty snaps or cords.
- **Normal wear:** pilling, abrasion from pack straps, fading, water-repellent coating wearing off (restore it with a wash-in treatment).
- **Not covered:** burns, tears from snagging, damage from washing against the care label.

## Bags and backpacks
- **Defect:** seams or buckles failing under normal loads, broken frame stays, failing zips, waterproof bags leaking at the seams.
- **Normal wear:** abrasion on the base, faded fabric, foam back panels compressing.
- **Not covered:** damage from airline handling or from overloading past the stated capacity.

## Skis and snowboards
- **Defect:** delamination, core cracks without impact, edges separating from the base.
- **Normal wear:** base scratches, edge burrs, top-sheet chips, loss of camber over seasons.
- **Not covered:** damage from rocks, rails or collisions, or problems caused by mounting bindings.

## Goggles
- **Defect:** frame cracks without impact, foam detaching, anti-fog layer failing when new.
- **Not covered:** lens scratches, or fogging caused by wiping the inside of the lens.

## Climbing gear
- **Defect:** stitching or buckles failing on an unused or lightly used harness, a crampon or ice axe cracking under normal use.
- **Normal wear:** rope sheath fuzz, harness fading or abrasion, dull crampon points and axe picks (sharpen them).
- Retire any climbing gear after a significant fall or impact. This is not covered by warranty.

## Helmets
- **Defect:** buckles or the fit dial failing, padding detaching.
- **Not covered:** any impact damage. See **Crash Replacement** in **Warranty Overview**.

## Tents, poles and headlamps
- **Defect:** pole sections cracking under normal pitching, floor seams leaking when new, headlamps failing without water damage.
- **Normal wear:** UV fading, small abrasions, reduced battery life after several years.

## GPS watches, navigators and bike computers
- **Defect:** screen, button or charging failure in normal use, battery that will not hold a charge in the first year.
- **Not covered:** cracked screens, water damage beyond the stated water rating, gradual battery ageing.
