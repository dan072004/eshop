# Sizing Guide: Helmets, Packs and Harnesses

## Bike helmets
Measure around your head about **1 inch (2.5 cm) above your eyebrows**, keeping the tape level.

| Size | Head circumference |
|---|---|
| S | 51–55 cm (20–21.7 in) |
| M | 55–59 cm (21.7–23.2 in) |
| L | 59–63 cm (23.2–24.8 in) |

A helmet fits when it sits level, about two finger widths above your eyebrows, and does not move when you shake your head with the strap fastened. The side straps should form a **V just below your ears**. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller size and use the fit dial. Worn helmets cannot be returned; see **Returns Exceptions**.

## Backpacks: torso length
Pack size depends on your **torso length**, not your height. Ask someone to measure from the bony bump at the base of your neck (C7 vertebra) down your spine to the line between the tops of your hip bones.

| Pack size | Torso length |
|---|---|
| S | 15–17 in (38–43 cm) |
| M | 17–19 in (43–48 cm) |
| L | 19–21 in (48–53 cm) |

The hip belt should sit on top of your hip bones, carrying most of the weight. Packs listed as one size have an adjustable back for torso lengths of **16–20 in (41–51 cm)**.

## Backpacks: volume
| Trip | Volume |
|---|---|
| Day hike | 15–30 L |
| Overnight to 2 nights | 30–50 L |
| 3–5 nights | 50–70 L |
| Travel carry-on | 35–45 L (check your airline's limits) |

## Climbing harnesses
Harnesses are sized by **waist** and **leg loop** measurements. Measure your waist just above the hip bones, where the harness sits, and your thigh at the top of the leg.

| Size | Waist | Leg loops |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 24–28 in (61–71 cm) | 18–21 in (46–53 cm) |
| S | 27–31 in (69–79 cm) | 20–23 in (51–58 cm) |
| M | 31–35 in (79–89 cm) | 22–25 in (56–64 cm) |
| L | 35–39 in (89–99 cm) | 24–27 in (61–69 cm) |
| XL | 39–43 in (99–109 cm) | 26–29 in (66–74 cm) |

The waist belt must sit above your hip bones and must not slide over them when you hang upside down. Leave room for the layers you will climb in. Try your harness on indoors and keep the tags on: used harnesses cannot be returned.

## Trekking poles
Adjustable poles fit most people. Set the length so your elbow is at **90 degrees** when the tip is on the ground beside your foot. Shorten them by about 5 cm for long climbs and lengthen them for descents.
