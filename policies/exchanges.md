# Exchanges

## What you can exchange
You can exchange an item for a **different size or colour of the same product** within **60 days** of the order date. The item must meet the condition rules in the **Returns Policy** and any rules in **Returns Exceptions**.

To swap for a different product, return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Cost
- Your **first exchange per item is free**: free return label and free Standard shipping on the replacement.
- After the first exchange, the **$6.95** return label fee applies, and the replacement ships at the normal Standard rate.

## How it works
1. Email support@adventureworks.example with your order number (shown in **My orders**), the item, and the new size or colour you want.
2. We reserve the replacement for **14 days**.
3. The replacement ships as soon as the carrier **scans your return**. You do not have to wait for it to reach our warehouse.

If you do not send the original item within **14 days**, the exchange is cancelled and the reserved item is released.

## When the replacement is out of stock
If the size or colour you want is out of stock, we cannot hold an exchange. Return the item for a refund instead, and order the new size once it is back in stock.

## Price differences
Exchanges are for the same product, so there is no price difference. If the product's price has changed since you bought it, you keep the price you paid.
