# Sizing Guide: Skis and Snowboards

These guides give a starting point. Lengths on each product page show what is available.

## Ski length by height and ability
Our skis are all-mountain skis. Pick a length based on your height, then adjust for ability.

| Your height | Beginner | Intermediate | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5'0" (152 cm) | 135–145 cm | 145–150 cm | 150–155 cm |
| 5'4" (163 cm) | 145–155 cm | 155–160 cm | 160–165 cm |
| 5'8" (173 cm) | 155–165 cm | 165–170 cm | 170–175 cm |
| 6'0" (183 cm) | 165–175 cm | 175–180 cm | 180–185 cm |
| 6'4" (193 cm) | 175–180 cm | 180–185 cm | 185–190 cm |

- **Beginners:** a ski reaching between your chin and nose is easier to turn.
- **Advanced skiers:** a ski reaching your forehead or above is more stable at speed.
- If you weigh more than average for your height, go a size longer; if less, a size shorter.

## Snowboard length by weight
Weight matters more than height, because it decides how the board flexes.

| Rider weight | Board length |
|---|---|
| 90–110 lb (41–50 kg) | 138–144 cm |
| 110–130 lb (50–59 kg) | 144–150 cm |
| 130–150 lb (59–68 kg) | 150–154 cm |
| 150–170 lb (68–77 kg) | 154–158 cm |
| 170–190 lb (77–86 kg) | 158–162 cm |
| 190–220 lb (86–100 kg) | 162–166 cm |

- **Freestyle and park:** go 2–4 cm shorter for easier spins and presses.
- **Powder:** go 2–4 cm longer for more float.
- **Width:** if your boots are US men's size 11 or larger, choose a **wide** (W) board so your toes and heels do not drag.

## Snowboard boot size (mondopoint)
Snowboard boots use **mondopoint**: your foot length in centimetres. Measure your foot as described in **Sizing Guide: Jackets and Footwear**, then pick the boot with that mondopoint.

| Mondopoint | US men | US women |
|---|---|---|
| 24.5 | 6.5 | 8 |
| 25.5 | 7.5 | 9 |
| 26.5 | 8.5 | 10 |
| 27.5 | 9.5 | 11 |
| 28.5 | 10.5 | 12 |
| 29.5 | 11.5 | — |

New boots should feel snug. They pack out by about half a size in the first few days on snow.

## Goggles
Goggles come in one size. If you wear a helmet, check that the strap fits around it and that there is no gap between the goggle frame and the helmet brim.
