# Returns Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within **60 days** of the order date for a full refund. The order date is the date you placed the order, shown in **My orders**. For example, an order placed on October 3 can be returned until December 2.

## Condition of returned items
Returned items must be:
- Unused, unwashed and free of wear outside the home.
- In their original packaging, with all tags, labels and accessories attached.

Items that come back used or incomplete may be refused or refunded only in part. Some categories have stricter rules; see **Returns Exceptions**.

## How to start a return
1. Email support@adventureworks.example with your order number (shown in **My orders**) and the items you want to return.
2. We reply within 1 business day with a prepaid return label.
3. Print the label and drop the parcel at any carrier location.

You can only return an order once its status is **Shipped**. If your order has not been paid yet, cancel it instead; see **Order Cancellation**.

## Return shipping cost
- **Refunds:** a **$6.95** return label fee is deducted from your refund.
- **Exchanges:** return shipping is **free**. See **Exchanges**.
- **Defective or wrong items:** return shipping is **free**, and no fee is deducted.

Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.

## Refunds
We refund the original payment card (Visa, Mastercard or Amex) within **5–7 business days** after your return arrives at our warehouse. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit. We cannot refund to a different card.

## Gift returns
Items bought as gifts can be returned within the same **60 days** for an AdventureWorks gift card for the item's value. The person who placed the order is not notified.
