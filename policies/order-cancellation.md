# Order Cancellation and Changes

## When you can cancel
You can cancel an order **until it is paid**. That means while its status is **Submitted**, **Awaiting validation** or **Stock confirmed**.

Once an order is **Paid** or **Shipped**, it can no longer be cancelled. Wait for it to arrive and return it under the **Returns Policy** instead.

Payment usually happens within minutes of checkout, so cancel as soon as possible.

## How to cancel
1. Sign in and open **My orders**.
2. Find the order and select **Cancel**.

The **Cancel** option only appears while the order can still be cancelled. If it is missing, the order has already been paid.

## What happens to your money
We charge your card only after stock is confirmed. If you cancel before the order is paid, **you are not charged**. You may see a temporary authorisation from your bank, which disappears within **3–5 business days**.

## Orders we cancel
We cancel an order automatically, and you are not charged, when:
- **Any item is out of stock.** The whole order is cancelled, not just the missing item. Place a new order for the items that are in stock.
- **Your payment is declined.** See **Payments and Billing**.

You will see the status change to **Cancelled** in **My orders**.

## Changing an order
We cannot change an order once it is placed, including:
- the shipping address
- items, sizes or quantities
- the shipping speed

To make a change, cancel the order while it is still possible and place a new one.
