# Shipping Options and Costs

## Shipping speeds
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–7 business days | $6.95, **free on orders over $75** |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $14.95 |
| Next day | 1 business day | $29.95 |

Delivery times start when the order **ships**, not when you place it. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays.

## Free shipping over $75
Standard shipping is free when your order total is **over $75** after discounts and before tax. Free shipping does not apply to Expedited or Next day, or to the oversized surcharge on skis and snowboards.

## When your order ships
Orders that are **paid by 12 pm Pacific Time** on a business day ship the same day. Orders paid after 12 pm PT, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

We only ship after stock is confirmed and payment succeeds. See **Order Status and Tracking**.

## Split shipments
We usually send an order in one parcel. If we split it, you will not pay extra, and you will get a tracking number for each parcel.

## Where we ship
We ship to the United States and Canada. Some options are not available everywhere; see **Shipping Destinations**.

## Oversized items
Skis and snowboards have a **$25** surcharge and ship by Standard only. See **Oversized Shipping**.
