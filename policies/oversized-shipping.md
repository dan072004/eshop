# Oversized Shipping

## Which items are oversized
**Skis and snowboards** are oversized. Snowboard boots, goggles, tents, backpacks and duffels ship as normal parcels.

## Surcharge
Each ski pair or snowboard has a **$25** oversized surcharge.
- The surcharge applies **per item**: two snowboards cost $50.
- It applies even when Standard shipping is **free over $75**. Free shipping covers the normal Standard rate, not the surcharge.

## Shipping speed
Oversized items ship by **Standard only** (3–7 business days). Expedited and Next day are not available for orders that contain skis or snowboards.

If your order mixes oversized and normal items, you can still choose Expedited or Next day. The normal items ship at that speed; the skis or snowboards follow separately by Standard.

## Where oversized items can go
We cannot deliver skis or snowboards to **PO boxes**, APO/FPO addresses or parcel lockers. See **Shipping Destinations**.

## Packaging
Skis and snowboards are shipped in a reinforced box with edge protection. Keep the box for **60 days** in case you need to return the item. Returns without suitable packaging may be refused if the item is damaged in transit.

## Bindings
We sell skis and snowboards **without bindings**, and we do not mount bindings. Drilled binding holes make a ski or snowboard non-returnable; see **Returns Exceptions**.
